Fuel & Training

Log what you eat and how you move each day, and see whether you landed in a calorie deficit. Numbers are estimates — chase the weekly pattern, not a perfect day.

A moderate deficit is the healthy one. A surplus day isn't a failure — it's data. If tracking ever feels stressful, or you notice yourself eating too little, step back. That's the smart move, not weakness.

BMI is instant from weight/height. Body fat uses the US Navy method — waist, neck (and hip for females) — a fixed formula, so identical measurements always give the same number. Both are estimates, not lab-grade scans; measure at the same time of day for consistency.
